2. Route by customer outcome

Not every negative review is a removal candidate.

Controlled
  • Clear policy signal: human policy validation, then approvals.
  • Product feedback: quality or listing-improvement task.
  • Delivery or seller service: operations or customer-experience follow-up.
  • Unclear: hold for a policy owner.

Not connected
