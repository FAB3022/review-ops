1. Collect and validate input

Google Sheet rows are checked before entering the queue.

Works now
  1. Paste exact source fields into Review Intake.
  2. Required-column and duplicate checks identify rows that need correction.
  3. Export the ready rows as CSV and import them here.

2. Route by customer outcome

Not every negative review is a removal candidate.

Controlled
  • Clear policy signal: human policy validation, then approvals.
  • Product feedback: quality or listing-improvement task.
  • Delivery or seller service: operations or customer-experience follow-up.
  • Unclear: hold for a policy owner.

3. Add independent sync later

Requires an approved endpoint and access owner.

Not connected
  1. Apps Script runs on a schedule and reads rows marked Ready.
  2. It sends only the approved fields to a secured internal endpoint.
  3. The endpoint returns a receipt and the Sheet records the sync result.

A view link exposes the sheet to anyone who has it; the Command Center build replaces this with a private connection. Do not automate Amazon submission unless Amazon provides an authorized route.
